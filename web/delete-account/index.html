Delete your Laundry Mate account

Sign in to confirm it's you, then permanently delete your account.

What happens when you delete

Shop owners: your login and your entire shop are deleted, including:

  • orders, payments and customer records
  • services and prices, machines, inventory and expenses
  • all attendant (staff) accounts you created

Customer tracking links and QR codes for your shop stop working.

Attendants: only your own login is deleted. Your shop and its orders stay with the owner.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Deleting from inside the app: Settings → Delete account.
Can't sign in? Email from the address on your account and ask us to delete it.
