Privacy Policy

WashNFold · Last updated: October 9, 2026

1. Who we are

WashNFold is a laundry shop management app (Android app and web app) used by shop owners and their staff to take orders, run the queue, track washers and dryers, and see sales. It is provided by [YOUR NAME / BUSINESS NAME] ("we", "us"). You can reach us at [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].

This policy explains what information the app handles, why, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it.

2. Information we handle

WhoseWhatWhy
Shop owners and staff Email address and password (stored as a secure hash, we never see your password), your name, and your role (owner or attendant). To create your account, sign you in, and decide what you can see.
The shop Shop name, services and prices, washers and dryers and their timers, inventory, and expenses. To run the shop in the app and show reports to the owner.
The shop's customers (entered by the shop) Customer name, optional phone number, loyalty points, and their orders: items, totals, status, dates, payment method and amount, and a tracking reference. To process and track laundry orders and let customers check their order status.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, and the app does not contain advertising or third-party analytics or tracking tools.

3. Customer order tracking page

A shop can show a QR code that opens a public tracking page. Customers type the reference printed on their receipt and see only the shop name, the order's current status (such as Washing or Ready) and its basic dates. Customers do not need an account and are not asked for personal details on that page. As with any website, our hosting providers may keep standard technical logs such as IP address and browser type.

4. How we use information

  • To provide, secure and support the app.
  • To let owners manage their shop, staff and reports.
  • To let customers check their own order status.
  • To send you account-related messages, such as password reset.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Shop owners and their customers' data

Customer names, phone numbers and orders are entered by the shop and belong to that shop. Shop owners decide what to collect from their own customers and are responsible for using it lawfully. We process that data only to provide the app to the shop. If you are a customer and want your details corrected or removed, please ask the laundry shop directly.

6. Who we share information with

We use service providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf and only to provide their service:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, and server functions.
  • Vercel: hosting of the web app and the public pages.

Within a shop, owners can see everything for their shop, while attendants see only what the app allows them to use. We may also disclose information if required by law.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When an account is deleted (see below), the data described there is removed. Copies held in our providers' backups may remain for a short time before they are overwritten.

8. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account.
  • On the web: washnfold.app/delete-account

Shop owners: deleting your account permanently deletes your login and your whole shop: orders, customers, services, machines, inventory, expenses, and all staff accounts you created.

Attendants: deleting your account removes your login. Your shop and its orders remain with the owner; your name may remain on past orders as a record of who handled them.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you cannot sign in, email us at [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] from the address on the account.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored hashed, and the database restricts each account to its own shop's data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep it private.

10. Children

WashNFold is a business tool for shop owners and staff. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Your choices

You can ask us to access or correct the information tied to your account, or delete it, using the contact above or the deletion options in section 8.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
