Features

Everything a laundry counter needs

From the moment a customer drops off a bag to the moment they pick it up, every step is in one place.

New orders in seconds

Pick the services, enter the weight or number of pieces, and the total is worked out for you, including per-kg pricing and add-ons.

A live queue

Orders move from Received to Washing, Drying, Folding, Ready and Picked up, and every device sees the change as it happens.

Washers & dryers

Assign loads to machines, watch the countdown, and send finished loads on to drying or folding with one tap.

Customer tracking

Every order gets a reference and a tracking link or QR code, so customers can check on their laundry without calling the shop.

Payments

Record cash, GCash, Maya or card payments, or let the customer pay at pickup. Unpaid orders are always easy to spot.

Reports for owners

See sales for today, this week and this month, plus daily, weekly and custom reports. Attendants only see the work queue.

Up and running in three steps
